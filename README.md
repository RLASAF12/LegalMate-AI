## The Problem

Legal questions don't come up on a schedule. They come up at 11 PM when you're reviewing a contract, on Sunday when a client threatens to sue, or right before you sign a lease. Lawyers charge **$300-700/hour** and don't respond on weekends.

**After 6 years of answering the same legal questions from business owners — "Can they do this?", "Am I liable?", "What does this clause mean?" — I realized most of these answers could be delivered instantly by AI.**

## The Solution

LegalMate AI is a conversational legal assistant that answers business law questions in plain, understandable language. It covers the topics business owners actually ask about: contracts, IP, employment law, privacy, business formation, and commercial disputes.

---

## Features

- **Conversational Chat**: Ask legal questions naturally — get structured, helpful answers
- **6 Legal Categories**: Business Formation, Contracts, Intellectual Property, Employment Law, Privacy & Data Protection, Commercial Disputes
- **Plain Language Responses**: No legalese — explanations written for business owners, not lawyers
- **Context-Aware**: Full conversation history so follow-up questions build on prior answers
- **Popular Topics Library**: Pre-built Q&A for the most common business law questions
- **Suggested Follow-ups**: 3 relevant follow-up questions after each answer to explore deeper
- **Chat History**: Save, revisit, and continue conversations anytime
